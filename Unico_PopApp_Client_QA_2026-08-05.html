Summary

A guest customer can complete a purchase, but only by guessing. Four defects combine into a checkout that silently refuses to submit and never says why.

I reserved a ticket for EVENT_STARTING_SOON ($10, package T1) and paid via Pay in Person. The order was created successfully (POST /app/event/app/order/event/create → 200, order #274049), the ticket was issued with a valid QR pass, and stock decremented correctly from 9 to 8 spots. Everything around that happy path, however, has problems.

The most serious cluster is on the checkout screen: Place Order fails silently. There is no toast, no inline error, and no field highlighting — and no network request is even attempted. I hit this twice and had to diagnose it from the DOM. The two hidden causes are a required consent checkbox that silently resets itself and an "Order Note" field that is enforced as required while being labelled optional. A real customer would very likely abandon here.

The storefront

For context — this is what a customer sees on arrival.

High severity

These block or mislead a paying customer.

High

1 · "Place Order" fails silently with no feedback whatsoever

What happens

Pressing Place Order with any validation problem does nothing visible. No toast, no inline error message, no red field outlines, no scroll-to-error on the second attempt. The button remains fully styled as enabled. Confirmed via the network panel that no request is sent at all — the submit is rejected client-side and swallowed.

Why it matters

This is the failure that makes issues 2 and 3 dangerous. Individually they'd be minor annoyances; combined with zero feedback, the customer is left tapping a dead button with no path forward. Expected behaviour: block submission and say which field is wrong.

Repro

1. Reserve a ticket → checkout
2. Leave the consent checkbox unticked (or the Order Note empty)
3. Tap "Place Order"
→ nothing happens; no message; no network call
High

2 · The required consent checkbox silently resets itself

What happens

I ticked the required agreement, then filled in the Order Note. The act of filling the note cleared the tick, with no indication. Pressing Place Order then failed silently (issue 1).

Why it matters

This creates an unwinnable loop: the customer fixes one field, unknowingly loses their consent, submits, gets nothing, and has no way to work out what changed. It is almost certainly the root cause of the "Place Order does nothing" reports.

Repro

1. On checkout, tick the required agreement checkbox
2. Type anything into the Order Note field
3. Scroll back up
→ the agreement checkbox is unticked again
High

3 · Order Note is enforced as required but labelled "optional" — twice

What happens

The field header reads Order Note * with a red required asterisk, while the sub-label says "Optional note to merchant" and the placeholder says "Leave a message for the merchant (optional)". Submission is genuinely blocked until it is filled.

Why it matters

The UI actively tells the customer they may skip a field that will then block their purchase — and, thanks to issue 1, without explaining itself. This is the customer-side confirmation of the "Mandatory Order Note vs (optional) placeholder" item already logged against the merchant dashboard.

High

4 · Pay-in-Person orders hang on "verifying your payment" forever

What happens

After ordering with Pay in Person ("Pay at check-in"), the confirmation screen shows a PAYMENT PROCESSING badge and the text "We're verifying your payment. You'll be redirected to your tickets once it's confirmed."

There is no payment to verify — the customer is paying at the venue. Worse, order/event/info?id=274049 is called exactly once, with no polling and no socket subscription, so the promised redirect can never fire. Re-loading the page later shows the identical stuck state.

Why it matters

The customer is told their payment is being verified when nothing is pending, and is promised a redirect that will never arrive. Both the copy and the state machine are wrong for offline payment methods.

High

5 · The legal agreement is labelled with a raw image filename

What happens

The required consent checkbox — the one gating the purchase — is labelled Group 1000004246.png. The merchant's terms were uploaded as an image (a 5560×4248 PNG on S3) and the label falls back to the file's name.

Why it matters

Customers are being asked to formally agree to something identified only by a filename. Beyond looking broken, consent to an unnamed document is difficult to defend. The label should render the terms' title, with the document behind a "view" affordance. Storing terms as a single enormous image is also worth revisiting — it is unreadable on a phone, unsearchable, and inaccessible to screen readers.

Medium severity

Degraded experience or wrong information, but not blocking.

Medium

6 · The agreement label is a link, so it cannot be ticked by tapping it

Clicking anywhere on the agreement text opens the terms image in a new tab instead of toggling the checkbox. Only the ~14 px square itself works — a hostile target on a phone, and the opposite of the near-universal convention that a checkbox label toggles its box.

Medium

7 · Uncaught TypeError thrown on every single page load

Every page load raises an uncaught exception during app bootstrap:

TypeError: service.request is not a function
    at createEps (cool/bootstrap/eps.ts:86)
    at bootstrap (cool/bootstrap/index.ts:7)
    at createApp (main.js:16)

The app recovers and renders, but an exception on the bootstrap path is fragile and buries genuine errors in the console. This is the buyer-side counterpart to the Firebase messaging exception already logged against the merchant dashboard.

Medium

8 · Attendee name pre-filled with the internal guest code

On a fresh guest session the "Full name" field on the ticket-holder form arrives pre-filled with the internal account code — UC_m_6976. Left untouched, the ticket is issued in that name and the merchant receives it as the attendee.

The repo's own e2e/README.md already documents this fallback ("游客账号自带 nickName（UC_m_xxxx），姓名链路会兜底到它"), so it is known internally — but it reaches the customer. The field should start empty with a placeholder.

Medium

9 · The same order reports three different statuses

Order #274049, unchanged, described three different ways depending on where you look:

ScreenStatus shown
Order confirmationPAYMENT PROCESSING / "verifying your payment"
Event passPending Payment / PENDING PASS
Order listPending Confirmation

Same defect class as the refund-status contradiction already logged on the merchant side: no single source of truth for order state.

Medium

10 · The service fee silently appears and disappears with payment method

With Online Payment selected the total is $10.40 (ticket $10.00 + service fee $0.40). Switching to Pay in Person drops the total to $10.00 and removes the "Service fee" line entirely, with no explanation.

Charging a processing fee only on card payments is reasonable; changing the price as a silent side effect of a radio button is not. It also means the sticky footer total can differ from the fee breakdown until the customer scrolls down to find it.

Low severity

Polish, placeholder data, and dead-end interactions.

Low

11 · Store name renders literally as "name"

The store's name field contains the literal string name, so the browser tab title and the event pass header both display "NAME" rather than "EVENTTEST01". Likely test-data hygiene rather than a code defect — but the pass header is customer-facing, so a fallback for an empty or placeholder store name would be worth having.

Low

12 · "Reserve Tickets" is a silent no-op when no package is selected

On the event detail page the primary CTA renders fully enabled (solid, high-contrast) but does nothing until a package is ticked. The only cue is small grey "SELECT A PACKAGE" text beside it. The button should be visually disabled, or should say what is missing.

Low

13 · Guest modal's "Continue" throws away the action you asked for

Tapping Wallet as a guest raises "You are currently browsing as a guest… sign in for the full experience" with Continue / Sign In Now. Choosing Continue dismisses the modal and leaves you on Profile — Wallet never opens. The intent is dropped, so "Continue" reads as "cancel".

Low

14 · Categories page renders a broken empty layout

The Categories tab renders an empty left sidebar column plus a content column with its own stray scrollbar, and no empty state. For a store with no categories it should show the standard "No data available" treatment used elsewhere.

What worked

Worth recording, so a re-test can tell regressions from known issues.

  • Order creation — POST /app/event/app/order/event/create returned 200 and produced order #274049.
  • Ticket issuance — a valid QR pass was generated (check-in ID 4898) with the correct guest name, pass type, date and check-in time.
  • Inventory — available spots decremented correctly from 9 to 8 after purchase.
  • Guest session — visitor token issued automatically; no forced sign-up before browsing or checkout.
  • Fee disclosure — the $0.40 service fee is itemised under "Price details" before payment.
  • Tickets archive — renders correctly with valid/used/all filters and a sensible empty state.

Suggested priority

  1. Give Place Order a voice. Fixing issue 1 alone converts three silent blockers into ordinary, recoverable form errors. Highest value per unit of effort.
  2. Stop the consent checkbox resetting (issue 2) — this is the actual blocker behind abandoned checkouts.
  3. Make Order Note's label match its behaviour (issue 3) — one-line fix, either direction.
  4. Branch the confirmation screen on payment method (issue 4), and add polling or a socket update for the paths that genuinely are pending.
  5. Render a real title for the terms agreement (issues 5 & 6) and make the label toggle the box.

Scope and method. Tested against the test backend (api.unico-app.com/coolAdminTest) via the H5 build served by HBuilderX, browsing as an unauthenticated guest. One real order was placed on the test store to exercise the full path; it can be cancelled or left as a fixture. Not covered: the Zelle/Venmo and Finix card payment paths (no card details were entered), refund and cancellation flows, coupon redemption, points, and any non-H5 target (iOS, Android, mini-program).
